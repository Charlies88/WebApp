Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Snazzy Paws Grooming collects, uses, and protects your personal information when you use our website, in line with the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020.

Information We Collect

  • Contact details you give us (name, email, phone) when booking or contacting us
  • Basic usage data (pages visited, browser type) collected automatically
  • Cookies may be used to remember preferences and improve the site

How We Use Your Information

  • To manage bookings and respond to enquiries
  • To improve our website and services
  • To contact you about your booking or our services

Do You Have to Provide Information?

Providing your contact details is optional, but if you don't, we may not be able to process your booking or respond to your enquiry.

Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share it with service providers who help us run the website (e.g. booking form or hosting providers), or where required by law.

Data Retention

We keep your information only as long as needed for the purpose it was collected, then delete or anonymise it.

Your Rights

Under the Privacy Act 2020, you can ask to see, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you. Contact us using the details below to make a request.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us via our Contact page.
